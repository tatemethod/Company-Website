About Fiona Tate

I've spent my career making complicated organisations work better, at the intersection of media, technology, operations and transformation.

What I've learned is that transformation rarely depends solely on finding the right technology or having the right strategy. The difficult bit is building an organisation capable of making it happen.

I founded The Method Consultancy to help leaders bridge the gap between the promise of technology, AI, and new operating models, and the reality of making them work at scale.

Experience & Background

Before establishing Method, I served as Global SVP of Operational Excellence at WPP Media. In this role, I oversaw global transformation initiatives and the adoption of artificial intelligence across more than 80 markets, supporting a network of over 40,000 practitioners.

Operating at this genuine global scale requires a structured, rigorous approach to problem-solving and governance. My methodology is rooted in analytical precision—supported by a First-Class Honours degree in Physics and formal certification as a PRINCE2 Practitioner—allowing me to translate high-level commercial ambitions into scalable, efficient operating models.

Today, I partner directly with Chief Marketing Officers, procurement leaders, and AdTech executives to design future-ready marketing organizations, implement outcome-based frameworks, and turn rapid industry change into a measurable competitive advantage.
